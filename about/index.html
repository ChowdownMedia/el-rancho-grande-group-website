Who we are

A Family Table

El Rancho Grande has been serving the community of Cincinnati & Dayton, OH since March of 1998. We are a family owned and operated business, and our menu offers an array of traditional Mexican specialties made with only the freshest ingredients. There is something for every taste and diet, including vegetarian & vegan options.

What started as a single neighborhood cantina has grown into a family of restaurants across the region, each one serving the same handcrafted margaritas, sizzling fajitas and made-from-scratch favorites that started it all.
